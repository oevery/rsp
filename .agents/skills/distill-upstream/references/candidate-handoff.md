# Candidate handoff

Use when returning a concrete RSP improvement option. Research does not authorize a Change, implementation, model execution or adoption.

For a candidate, explain:

- the local problem or opportunity and its observed location;
- the dated report and source commit/path or other locatable source supporting it;
- the smallest proposed change, conditions and unsuitable transfer;
- what should be checked before claiming improvement.

Existing historical or standalone reports can be cited with their actual limits; do not invent a sealed identity or required recommendation number. A new source version does not by itself justify changing a conclusion. Adoption belongs to the normal Change and product owners, not a research approval ledger.

For performance or stability claims, use comparable task inputs, configurations, outcomes, failures, judgments and costs. Token reduction alone is not quality improvement. Refer to existing experiment records without changing their original outcomes. Paid execution needs its own authority.
