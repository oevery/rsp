### MODIFIED
- Requirement: Owning Skills choose methods and interpret deterministic observations within current authority. Tool presence, version, exit or status recommendation supplies neither authority nor semantic acceptance. Preserve standalone packages and actual required owning capabilities.
- Requirement: A replaceable execution method may change inside the same owner only when goal, scope, authoritative baseline, permissions and required evidence remain unchanged. Named mandatory commands, provenance operations, independence and protected RSP mutations retain their declared owners; an alternative must not waive them.
- Requirement: Diagnose in-scope tool failures and inspect actual effects before recovery. Safe read-only or proven repeat-safe work may continue; attempted one-shot delivery, unsafe facts, unknown mutation, missing decisive evidence or a changed authority/evidence boundary require the corresponding stop or owner decision. No universal retry or Git-specific rule applies to all operations.
- Requirement: Doc optimization makes actions, prerequisites, success signals, conditional reading and artifact ownership findable without deleting substantive qualifiers. Review all twelve complete canonical packages and current effective document surfaces; record kept/changed decisions, not a change-per-file quota.

### Acceptance

#### Scenario: Recover a replaceable method

- GIVEN one authorized objective, a fixed evidence requirement and a diagnosed tool-only obstacle
- WHEN an equivalent permitted method is available with inspectable effects
- THEN the owning capability continues safely without inventing a new phase or changing acceptance.

#### Scenario: Preserve required evidence and safety

- GIVEN a named required check, independent reviewer, immutable source or protected mutation
- WHEN its evidence is unavailable or prior effects are uncertain
- THEN no tool switch, output label or author self-check grants completion or bypass authority.

#### Scenario: Follow the whole document surface

- GIVEN an installed standalone Skill or a maintainer reading current Specs and guidance
- WHEN selecting the next action and interpreting a failure
- THEN local instructions preserve the same ownership and evidence boundary, branch references are usable and current facts do not depend on historical research or this session.

## Design
One consistency outcome spans the same source review and verification boundary. Keep independent capability owners rather than splitting by package or introducing a shared runtime policy dependency. Design owns the stable agent/tool boundary; Skill and Core carry their responsibility projections; each standalone package retains its necessary local safety context.

Use rsp-doc's Skill, agent-facing and artifact methods. Inspect each complete package and all current formal Specs and guides; improve evidenced decision and reading difficulties, retaining adequate content. Audit command entrypoints and root scripts for mechanical versus semantic responsibility. Change executable behavior only for an evidenced in-scope defect with focused public regression, not to make every tool optional.

Disjoint package/document writers may work concurrently under qualified Core coordination; main owns shared Specs, fallback, Core and verification resources. Independent reviewers receive a fixed pre-change snapshot and complete assigned packages. Reports and per-file dispositions stay under ignored tests/skills/reports/agent-first-consistency; the Change retains only final outcomes and decisive evidence.

Fresh model execution uses the shared gpt-6.1-sol medium executor and high judge in isolated projects. Select evidence-preserving recovery and restraint plus document/provenance tasks by risk; use the existing runner and real side-effect oracles. Historical matrices remain unchanged. No comparative quality/cost gain or complete release acceptance follows without appropriate evidence.
