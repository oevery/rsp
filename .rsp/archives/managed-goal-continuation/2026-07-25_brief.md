# Change Group: managed-goal-continuation

## Goal
- Continue bounded managed goals through reshaping, checkpoints, and opt-in push

## Scope
- Let one explicit managed goal continue across the smallest successive Change or shallow Group owners without adding nested Groups or durable controller state.
- Re-enter Shape when execution evidence reveals missing in-scope ownership, then re-check and resume without a redundant authorization round.
- Establish proactive local checkpoint commit behavior for managed continuation while keeping every push strictly opt-in and milestone-bound.
- Qualify the combined behavior with deterministic contracts and a fresh long-running host scenario containing short, sequential, parallel, and newly shaped work.

## Shared Constraints
- The initial managed request bounds product scope and planning-artifact mutation; material product, acceptance, external-action, or scope changes still stop at the owner.
- RSP Changes and Group Briefs remain durable truth. Goal selection, waves, dispatch, retry, and checkpoint chronology remain transient.
- Project or user instructions may deny automatic commits. Push, force-push, publication, deployment, approval, and human acceptance are never inferred.
- Do not create nested Groups, persistent WorkSets, controller state, receipts, or a second lifecycle.

## Slices
- `managed-goal-continuation/continue-across-owners`: continue clear managed goals across successive WorkRefs, stop naturally for terminal short work, and route missing owners through Shape before resuming.
- `managed-goal-continuation/checkpoint-delivery`: define safe model-selected local checkpoint commits and explicit-only milestone push authority without weakening repository Git rules.
- `managed-goal-continuation/validate-long-goal`: prove the integrated model on deterministic and fresh host evidence spanning parallel, sequential, reshaped, checkpointed, terminal, and push-withheld paths.
- `managed-goal-continuation/refresh-native-composition-evidence`: refresh the independent exact-package native composition evidence invalidated by the final Skill and fallback-rule changes.

## Completion Conditions
- [x] A clear managed goal can create or refine in-scope Change/Group owners during execution, re-derive readiness, and continue until the goal is complete or a real boundary stops it.
- [x] Small terminal work stops without orchestration churn; long work selects parallel or sequential execution from dependencies and mutation overlap.
- [x] Local commits occur only at clean independently reviewable checkpoints allowed by current instructions; push occurs only after explicit user authority and at a declared Group or goal milestone.
- [x] The complete product and exact-package contract passes, with retained real-host evidence and no unauthorized paths, push, publication, deployment, or persistent controller state.
- [x] Both managed-goal behavior and the pre-existing native-design composition surface are freshly qualified against the same final authored package state.

## Durable Outcomes
- Current facts: `.rsp/specs/design.md`, `docs/design-philosophy.md`, and the published Skill contracts.
- Lasting rationale: no separate Decision Record is needed; the commit, push, owner-transition, and wave-selection choices are operational product policy captured by the existing design surfaces rather than a hard-to-reverse architectural decision.

## Blockers
- none
