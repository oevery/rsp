# Change Group: managed-recovery-hardening

## Goal
- Make managed interruption and archived Group recovery unambiguous

## Scope
- Add an explicit, immutable-history-preserving lifecycle path for reopening a closed Change Group before reopening one of its archived children.
- Make managed pause, status, release, blocker, and resume semantics preserve one recoverable owner without persisting controller state.

## Shared Constraints
- Keep persisted lifecycle states limited to `open` and `archived`; do not add a paused state, controller ledger, or persisted execution chronology.
- Keep Group and child reopen explicit and separate. Never cascade into children or dependents, and never infer Git or external authority.
- Treat a status request as an update rather than a stop signal during authorized continuing work.
- Preserve immutable archives and fail before mutation on ambiguous history, malformed documents, collisions, or incomplete inspection.

## Slices
- `managed-recovery-hardening/reopen-closed-change-groups`: Add an explicit Group reopen lifecycle command and make grouped child reopen reachable without weakening fail-closed boundaries.
- `managed-recovery-hardening/strengthen-managed-pause-resume`: Define and validate managed status, pause, owner-release, blocker, and resume behavior around focused WorkRefs and response-only continuation.

## Completion Conditions
- [x] Both recovery capabilities are independently verified, reviewed, and archived.
- [x] Core, Manage, fallback rules, CLI documentation, and stable Specs present one consistent recovery model.
- [x] Full repository validation and retained behavior evidence pass without introducing controller state or external actions.

## Durable Outcomes
- Current facts: Updated `core-model.md`, `cli-contracts.md`, and `skill-system.md` with explicit Group reopen and managed interruption behavior.
- Lasting rationale: Updated the existing Group identity and immutable reopen Decisions for archive-bound Group recovery. Pause ownership remains a current operating fact in Core/Manage and Specs; no additional Decision Record was needed.
