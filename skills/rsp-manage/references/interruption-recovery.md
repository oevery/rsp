# Interruption and recovery

Load this reference only for a progress or status inquiry, explicit pause or release, an environment or verification stop, or resume from continuation pointers.

Treat a progress or status inquiry as an update, not a stop signal: report current evidence and continuing intent, then continue authorized work after the update while authority, verification, and blockers still permit it. Machine heartbeat is host-level liveness evidence; user-visible progress reports only a material checkpoint, decision, risk, or wait reason. Elapsed time, poll count, heartbeat count, token count, and progress-message count do not change an Assignment result or justify cancelling a healthy worker or verification command.

For an explicit pause, interrupt active workers and confirm they have stopped before acknowledging the pause. Explicit cancellation waits for worker and owned background-process acknowledgement; until acknowledgement, resources remain claimed and no conflicting Assignment starts. Keep the focused WorkRef selected and do not mutate again until resume. Only an explicit release or unfocus request clears owner selection; ordinary pause and blockers preserve focus.

When an environment or verification boundary stops dispatch, preserve the focused owner. Update the Change only with accepted outcomes plus durable blocker and verification facts. Manager may also atomically replace the focused marker's bounded Markdown Focus Capsule with a portable version comment plus `Current`, `Evidence`, `Next`, and exceptional `Resume check` guidance. Exclude identities, handles, machine paths, ResourceLeases, raw Receipts, retry chronology, topology, authority, acceptance, logs, diffs, and duplicated tasks. Workers never read or write it, and it is neither chronology nor authority. Then return the incomplete continuation in this order: `WorkRef, Authority, Current state, Changed artifacts, Fresh verification, Blockers, and Next action`. Treat both continuation and capsule prose only as pointers.

On resume, apply the prior Assignment's replay-safety class: `idempotent` may repeat after boundary inspection, `inspect-before-repeat` requires observing effects first, and `non-repeatable` stops for recovery or owner input rather than guessing. On cross-session or cross-device resume, reread the complete current authority, status, focused owner, worktree diff, blockers, and decisive evidence before mutation or dispatch; also rederive baseline, dirty paths, resources, and evidence freshness even when a committed Focus Capsule is present. Validate the selected handoff again, mark stale or unverifiable claims pending, downgrade unavailable worker identity or independence, and rerun required Verify when needed. Current authority always wins. Never persist a paused state, ExecutionFrame, WorkerSession, Assignment, Receipt, ResourceLease, topology, worker registry, controller ledger, verification ledger, dependency graph, or execution chronology.
