# Interruption and recovery

Load this reference only for a progress or status inquiry, explicit pause or release, an environment or verification stop, or resume from continuation pointers.

Treat a progress or status inquiry as an update, not a stop signal: report current evidence and continuing intent, then continue authorized work after the update while authority, verification, and blockers still permit it. Machine heartbeat is host-level liveness evidence; user-visible progress reports only a material checkpoint, decision, risk, or wait reason. Elapsed time, poll count, heartbeat count, token count, and progress-message count do not change an Assignment result or justify cancelling a healthy worker or verification command.

For an explicit pause, interrupt active workers and confirm they have stopped before acknowledging the pause. Before host-confirmed Assignment admission, caller cancellation may abandon creation, resume, or delivery without creating an accepted dispatch. After admission, cancelling the caller's own wait does not retract accepted work. Explicit cancellation waits for worker and owned background-process acknowledgement or observed settlement through the host's interrupt or cancel contract; until acknowledgement, resources remain claimed and no conflicting Assignment starts. A terminal message or partial output does not release resources while owned work remains live. Keep the focused WorkRef selected and do not mutate again until resume. Only an explicit release or unfocus request clears owner selection; ordinary pause and blockers preserve focus.

When an environment or verification boundary stops dispatch, preserve the focused owner. Update the Change only with accepted outcomes plus durable blocker and verification facts. Manager may also atomically replace the focused marker's short optional Markdown Focus Capsule. A valid v1 capsule permits only one leading version declaration, blank lines, exactly one non-empty single-line `Current`, `Evidence`, and `Next`, and at most one non-empty single-line `Resume check`; unknown non-empty lines or fields are invalid. The capsule is owned only by Manager. Exclude worker identities, handles, absolute or machine-specific execution paths, ResourceLeases, raw receipts, retry chronology, topology, authority, acceptance, logs, diffs, and duplicated tasks. Workers communicate through messages rather than the capsule; it is neither chronology nor authority. The focus marker path remains the sole selection truth. Then return the incomplete outer `ControlOutcome` with `status: waiting`, the next owner/action, and bounded recovery guidance. Treat both continuation and capsule prose only as pointers; response recovery is continuation guidance, not another state store.

For semantic execution rollover, stop only at a Manager-accepted meaningful checkpoint. Compress the accepted outcome and decisive evidence into existing owners when authorized, inspect the diff and ResourceLease state, return the minimal continuation, and rederive a fresh ExecutionFrame before more work. Do not copy controller chronology or claim that an unavailable prior WorkerSession continues. Replace a Focus Capsule atomically only at such a checkpoint, never after each tool call or worker message. An intermediate commit may include a portable capsule only under separate commit authority; cross-device availability additionally requires a separately authorized Git transfer followed by current-evidence rederivation. Archive or explicit unfocus removes the marker.

On resume, apply the prior Assignment's replay-safety class: `idempotent` may repeat after boundary inspection, `inspect-before-repeat` requires observing effects first, and `non-repeatable` stops for recovery or owner input rather than guessing. Same-session continuation may use an AssignmentDelta only when the host observes the same compatible WorkerSession and its immediately accepted Assignment or AssignmentDelta boundary; the resumed call is still a new WorkerInvocation with its own admission, cancellation, settlement, WorkerReceipt, and release observations. Worker or sender provenance proves neither authority nor admission; authority still comes from that current envelope. On cross-session or cross-device resume, session loss, or invalidated continuity, require a fresh WorkerSession and complete Assignment; reread the complete current authority, status, focused owner, checkout diff, blockers, and decisive evidence before mutation or dispatch. Also rederive baseline, dirty paths, resources, observed execution location, and evidence freshness even when a committed Focus Capsule is present. Validate the selected handoff again, mark stale or unverifiable claims pending, downgrade unavailable worker identity or independence, and rerun required Verify when needed. Runtime settlement without a schema-valid required WorkerReceipt and Manager-derived AcceptedLaneEvidence keeps acceptance incomplete; partial or empty terminal output is recovery evidence only. Current authority always wins. Resume does not convert transient control objects into durable state.

If incomplete archived child acceptance belongs to a closed Group, recovery requires separate explicit lifecycle authority for `rsp group reopen <group> --reason <text>` before `rsp reopen <group>/<child>`. Restore neither children nor dependents implicitly.
