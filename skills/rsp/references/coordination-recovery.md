# Coordinated interruption and recovery

Load this reference only for a progress or status inquiry, explicit pause, an environment or verification stop, or resume from continuation pointers.

Reread the complete owner and authority only on a real invalidation, recovery, cross-session continuation, or closeout boundary.

Answer status inquiries with current evidence and continuing intent, then continue while authority, verification and blockers permit. Status inquiries do not pause work. Host liveness and elapsed time neither change a Discipline result nor justify cancelling healthy work.

For an explicit pause, interrupt through the host and confirm active workers or owned background processes have stopped before acknowledging it. Until then, start no conflicting mutation or verification. A cancelled wait, terminal message or partial output proves no stop.

Preserve the focused owner (`WorkOwner`) and focused child WorkRef when applicable during ordinary pause or blockers. Only an explicit release or unfocus request, archive, or another authorized lifecycle action changes selection. Update the Change only with accepted outcomes, decisive verification, and durable blockers.

When work needs a durable recovery snapshot, a Focus Capsule needs inspection or replacement, or a portable continuation handoff is required, read [focus and continuation recovery](focus-continuation.md). Same-session worker continuation alone does not require that reference. The capsule is a recovery pointer, never worker coordination, authority or acceptance.

## Resume from effects, not tool status

On same-session resume, reopen pointers to authority and owned artifacts, inspect drift and replay safety, and reassess decisive evidence applicability; rerun only missing or invalidated checks, not merely because work resumed.

Inspect actual effects before replay. An idempotent action may repeat after boundary inspection. An inspection-dependent action must first check prior effects; a non-repeatable action stops for recovery or owner input. Exit codes or partial results do not prove that nothing happened. Unknown mutation blocks replay; safe read-only observation may continue.

A permitted method change preserves authority, owner, scope, baseline and required evidence. Named checks, provenance and required independence remain binding. Once effects and host facts are established, [worker coordination](coordination.md) owns reuse or fresh dispatch; preserve the existing correction-loop counts and accepted outcomes across recovery.

For cross-session or cross-device recovery, distrust transient worker and liveness claims. Before mutation or delegation, reread current authority, selected owner, applicable tracking status, Git index, checkout diff, dirty paths, conflicting resources, blockers and execution location. Revalidate the selected handoff, decisive evidence and required verification against those facts; re-establish coordination qualification and host evidence for worker attribution or independence. Host completion without an attributable required worker result keeps acceptance incomplete. Current authority always wins.

For incomplete archived acceptance, read [reopen recovery](reopen-recovery.md) before any lifecycle mutation. It requires separate explicit lifecycle authority and owns exact archive selection and the closed-Group-before-child sequence. Restore neither children nor dependents implicitly.
