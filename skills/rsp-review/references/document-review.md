# Document review

Load for explanation or usage guidance within the fixed reviewed content, including Skill instructions and API/TSDoc comments. Apply the dimensions relevant to the reader's task; a short inline comment needs no synthetic introduction, document scaffold or standalone writing pass.

- Compare API comments and copyable examples with the smallest authoritative contract and implementation: inputs, outputs, failures, prerequisites and consequences.
- Label illustrative pseudocode and incomplete snippets; neither is a verified runnable recipe. A code fence or evidence-source read does not select Code by itself, and review grants no execution authority.
- Code handles applicable behavior/tool checks, not a duplicate writing pass.
- Skill guidance: descriptions discriminate neighboring intents; actions have prerequisites/results and conditional links have a reading purpose. Keep common safety and minimum actionable context local. Tables serve short stable mappings, not branching procedures; concise wording preserves substantive qualifiers.

Classify each document by its semantic role: requirement/Change, implementation plan, Spec, Decision Record/ADR, context/navigation, operating instruction, or explanatory/user documentation. Then check:

1. **Purpose:** identify the intended reader, available prior knowledge, and reading task. Missing framing matters when it causes a wrong decision or prevents that task, not merely when a preferred introduction is absent.
2. **Grounding:** verify authority and traceability of claims and decisions, including commands, defaults, conditions, limits, and failure outcomes. For shortened text, check lost negation, exceptions, permissions, quantities, units, certainty or necessary reasoning. Technical literals must not change under wording-only authority. Compare internal and cross-artifact meaning; polished prose can still make a false promise.
3. **Usability:** judge task usability separately from factual accuracy. Procedures need usable actions and success signals, contracts assessable conditions, and decisions reasons and tradeoffs. Against the intended reader's prior knowledge, check findability, prerequisites, definitions, causal and conditional links and visible unknowns without the author conversation. Retain mechanisms needed to reason, troubleshoot or judge guarantees; otherwise true internals may still leave the required action unclear. Preserve necessary teaching and local safety without demanding tutorials in references. Fixed order needs a correctness, safety or contract reason.
4. **Ownership:** check scope, duplicate authority, and choices disguised as settled decisions. An unresolved choice warrants an ambiguity Finding when it prevents the reviewed content from serving its declared task or safe use. Respect draft state and narrower review scope; intentionally deferred or out-of-scope choices are limits, not automatic defects.
5. **Maintenance:** check affected paths, links, anchors, terms and examples. For structural edits, follow reading paths and inspect moved content for omissions or broken ownership. Flag repeated examples or failure explanations only when they add no decision distinction. Preserve useful summaries, local safety and required evidence, including rare permission, serious-consequence and safe-recovery boundaries. An incident alone establishes no need for another constraint.

Check ownership by meaning, not file shape. CONTEXT owns canonical terms, domain relationships, and navigation; AGENTS owns operating rules; README owns introduction and usage. Specs own current contracts and necessary constraints, including design-reference tables when they define normative evidence. Change Spec owns only the delta and acceptance; Design owns the approach, Tasks the work, and Verify the evidence. Decision Records own rationale. Short summaries and references are valid; parallel full definitions are not.

Within fixed work-record scope, load the [current work record rules](../../rsp/references/work-model.md#current-work-records) when absent from context. Check that Tasks reflects current work, each Verify detail serves acceptance, applicability or necessary evidence lookup without repeating check results, and Focus contains only additional recovery context without a second state list or execution timeline. A record can still contain unnecessary execution detail without chronological headings. Preserve useful before/after comparisons, material failure dispositions and distinct review/acceptance boundaries. When judging blockers/readiness, compare actual impediments and the declaration with parsed state under the same work model. A claim of no blockers that parses as blocked is an inconsistency.

For closeout, inspect the final whole Change or archived artifact after needs-based writeback, not only added Verify lines or an earlier candidate. Check that any writeback uses verified results, necessary knowledge corrections are complete and the final Change reflects them without duplicating current specifications. Already-accurate knowledge needs no edit; required corrections cannot be waived. Whole-Change convergence removes stale operation permissions, attempt/session chronology and response-only handoff/readiness blocks while retaining the contract delta, material failures, version-bound results and evidence references. Return substantive inconsistencies to their owner; prose cleanup cannot resolve them. Do not hide failures, relabel reports or grant cleanup authority. CLI readiness cannot establish this semantic result.

Specialized Spec headings and omitted unnecessary scenarios are not defects; scaffold hints are optional writing aids, not requirements or evidence. Material promises, decisions, and limits must remain visible outside comments. Do not request extra sections, tests, or bulk historical rewrites merely for template conformity.

For document findings, identify the reader/task. For usability, identify what readers must infer and its effect on the task. An obscured action, scattered conditions, independent decisions crowded together or repetition can cause avoidable reconstruction or lookup. Cite the specific passage and effect; "too long" or "sounds generated" alone establishes no defect. Agreed expression rules are contracts; unagreed taste is not. Accept alternatives meeting the task and applicable contracts. Preserve helpful causal prose, navigation summaries and author voice. Keep contrasts resolving likely ambiguity; do not require one per rule or delete needed operations to shorten text. Length, list counts and format checks cannot replace judgment.

For example, an unexplained "the plan" can misdirect execution or force an avoidable search before the reader finds the right plan. A definition or prerequisite link that identifies it resolves that issue. Necessary prerequisite reading or a dense paragraph alone is no defect.

Reading difficulty is evidence to investigate. The five dimensions also guide writing, but Review uses this local reference and does not depend on Doc being installed. State whether reader comprehension was assessed locally or with actual independent evidence; never infer independence from a self-check or require a fixed number of rounds. Do not apply code-style or test-coverage rules to semantic documents.
