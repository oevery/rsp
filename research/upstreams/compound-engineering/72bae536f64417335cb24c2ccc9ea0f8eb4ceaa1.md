# Upstream Distillation: compound-engineering

## Source Position

- **Evidence:** The suite retains its broad ideation-to-delivery surface and adds two canonical Skills: ce-prototype for human-judged throwaway product experiments and ce-retune for measurement-first corpus retuning. Existing Skills gained cross-harness/model execution, paired old-versus-new evals, obligation deduplication, and portable script-path guidance (skills/**, docs/solutions/skill-design/**, tests/**).
- **Inference:** The revision strengthens evaluation methodology and adds useful comparison material for RSP design probes and Skill optimization, without changing the prior rejection of the suite-wide orchestration model.

## Extracted Mechanisms

- **M1 — Human-experienced prototype:** ce-prototype answers one expensive product question with a disposable artifact, stops in unattended mode, records decisions, and never ships the prototype.
- **M2 — Measurement-first retuning:** ce-retune requires a run archive, build selector, repeatable task, identical-arm noise floor, pre-registered bar, and separate process/task outcomes before editing a corpus.
- **M3 — Paired behavior comparison:** old/new corpus injection and deduplicated obligation scoring make regressions attributable rather than relying on prose inspection.
- **M4 — Cross-host dispatch remains adapter work:** model identity, argument rendering, script location, buffering, and sandbox behavior require explicit host handling.
