## Mechanisms

- Google Procedures: organize actual sequences as steps; name the action's purpose and execution context; include results when they help continuation. Avoid introductions that repeat headings and repeated procedures that can be linked.
- Google Lists: numbered lists convey meaningful order; bullets convey unordered peers. Use parallel item structure and explain whether all conditions apply.
- Microsoft: prefer precise familiar words and direct verbs; remove modifiers and phrases that add no meaning. Keep terms consistent without becoming abrupt.
- Diataxis: distinguish learning, doing, lookup and explanation. Match information to the reader's task instead of forcing all documents into one procedure.

## Disagreements and rejected transfer

Google Procedures uses a bullet for a single-step procedure while Lists generally discourages one-item lists. This is context-specific guidance, not a universal validation rule. RSP keeps necessary order and peer grouping without enforcing a count.

Reject imported English punctuation rules, fixed nesting styles, forbidden-word lists, a universal one-method policy, and compulsory Diataxis categories. Human explanations may need causal prose; that does not justify long execution instructions. Sparse Skill tables and stable symbol semantics come from RSP's selected contract and historical Changes, not these external guides.
