# Upstream Distillation: skillopt

## Source Position

- **Evidence:** SkillOpt treats Skill text as an optimizable artifact: task rollouts produce trajectories, reflection proposes edits, an optimizer selects changes, and a validation gate decides whether a candidate replaces the current best (README.md, docs/guide/training-loop.md, skillopt/**).
- **Evidence:** A separate SkillOpt-Sleep layer harvests agent history and exposes host-specific Skills/plugins for unattended recurring optimization (skillopt_sleep/**, plugins/**, docs/sleep/**).
- **Inference:** The controlled experiment and no-regression mechanisms are relevant; unattended self-modification conflicts with RSP publication authority.

## Extracted Mechanisms

- **M1 — Trajectory-driven edits:** failures and successes are reflected into bounded textual edits rather than replacing the whole Skill (docs/guide/training-loop.md, skillopt/optimizers/**).
- **M2 — Train/validation separation:** candidate updates pass through an explicit gate, and benchmark adapters keep data splits and rollout contracts separate (docs/reference/api.md, docs/guide/new-benchmark.md).
- **M3 — No-regression promotion:** the current best remains deployed when a candidate does not clear the validation rule; validation is more important than unconstrained generation (docs/sleep/RESULTS.md, skillopt_sleep/**).
- **M4 — Backends and environments are adapters:** model providers, target harnesses, datasets, and scoring environments have explicit interfaces (docs/guide/new-backend.md, docs/reference/config.md).

## Applicable to RSP

- Derive candidate edits from observed trajectories and specific failed expectations.
- Keep an immutable baseline/current-best candidate and require fresh holdout/no-regression evidence before promotion.
- Separate provider/harness adapters from the semantic Skill evaluation contract.
- Preserve rejected candidates and experiment receipts as research, not published behavior.

## Rejected

- Nightly, scheduled, or session-end automatic modification of RSP Skills.
- Harvesting user history without an explicit data and consent boundary.
- Deploying the statistically best candidate without qualitative authority review.
- Importing the optimizer stack into normal RSP workflow prose.

## Recommendations

- **R1 — Add candidate-versus-current no-regression gates (model-only).** A candidate advances only when required holdout behavior is not worse and the selected gap improves.
- **R2 — Record trajectory-linked edit rationale (model-only).** Every proposed instruction edit cites the failed or costly behavior it is intended to change.
- **R3 — Keep providers and harnesses behind evaluation adapters (independent-reimplementation).** Semantic cases remain host-neutral.
- **R4 — Prohibit unattended Skill publication (reject).** Automatic optimization may produce research candidates only.
