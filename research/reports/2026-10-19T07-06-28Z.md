## Upstream basis and limits

This implementation reuses the primary-source reading retained in the [earlier investigation](2026-10-19T06-05-36Z.md#primary-upstream-practices-and-their-limits). OpenAI's skill-evaluation guidance separates outcomes, process and other dimensions; Anthropic's agent-evaluation guidance warns against rigid paths/answers, recommends grading outcomes and calibrating judges with humans; Google's testing guidance favors behavior and refactor-resistant expectations. Their origins and capture identities remain locatable in that report and the existing research index. No new upstream refresh or code adoption is claimed.

The transfer is deliberately bounded: choose the grader for the question, admit valid alternatives, isolate negative controls and inspect real consequences. It does not justify arbitrary fuzzy thresholds, universal error/test/hash prohibitions, a mandatory workflow, or treating a second model's agreement as human truth.
