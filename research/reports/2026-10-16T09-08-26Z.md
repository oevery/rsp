# Frozen workflow candidate acceptance

All eleven selected scenarios have passing programmatic and semantic evidence
for the frozen candidate. Three jobs retain fifteen attempts: eleven passed,
three executor errors and one unscored judge result. Both required coverage gaps
are resolved; failures remain in their original records. Independent Code/Document
review is clean. The user confirmed closure, and the [Change was archived][archive]
on 2026-10-16; its Focus marker was cleared.

## Candidate and acceptance boundary

Candidate 45181b5feb7bb52f28231358f265f91e64392f41 combines workflow commit
178eaaae and evaluation/evidence commit 45181b5f over 78182947. The worktree was
clean at capture. The [freeze manifest][freeze] and [capture directory][capture]
retain source identity, commands, logs and input comparisons.

review-evidence-reuse, correction-review, writeback-in-progress,

Each job used one attempt per selected task, concurrency one and zero Harbor
retries. Both supplements had separate explicit authorization. No native job was
resumed or regraded; Codex's internal request retries remain visible in its logs.

All seven product Skills ran with executor AI-HUB/gpt-6.1-sol at medium effort and
judge openai/AI-HUB/gpt-6.1-sol at high effort. The rebuilt image identity is
sha256:3ddc67f3d297b870cd00f163704d4313dc78006b9c4f687f329b9fc2eaf3f23f,
linux/arm64. Runtime, injected inputs, executor/judge settings and image identity
were not tuned between attempts.

## Evidence applicability and retention

All 1,344 tracked files at freeze matched the tested cumulative source snapshot.
[Local evidence][local] supports the isolated product build, 21 CLI/Harbor tests,
full Harbor wrapper, final lint, package/document checks and 38 offline schemas.
The [architecture report][architecture] links the original build/type/code/security
coverage and affected reruns, including coverage of 69 code tests. Failed aggregates
remain failed; affected passes supplement them. No fresh full-suite execution is
claimed for this final acceptance.

Fresh live execution was necessary: the preceding outcome job retained the same
41 Skill files, but twelve task files and shared workspace support had changed.
Its old writeback native failure is not retroactively converted into a pass.

The [initial inputs][initial-inputs] contain 262 Skill/task/support files, all matched
to the source after execution. Each supplement's 87 selected files, dependency lock,
model catalog, effective configuration and exact image identity match the initial
capture. Only job identity, capture paths and task selection differ.

The [first supplement inputs][supp1-inputs] and [second supplement inputs][supp2-inputs]
remain separate. Preservation checks confirmed 1,049 original files unchanged after
the first supplement and 1,276 prior job/input files unchanged after the second;
see the [first][supp1-state] and [second][supp2-state] execution-state records.
HEAD and index stayed unchanged throughout acceptance. The only post-freeze delivery
changes were the Change/report and the two reviewed guide corrections; they do not
alter injected runtime or evaluation inputs.

Earlier experiments, failures and corrections remain in the [architecture and
follow-up report][architecture] and [initial evidence reconciliation][history].
Unlocated historical claims are excluded from acceptance. Native jobs, immutable
inputs, original scores, before images and review records are preserved in place.

product/evaluation candidate against 78182947. The [initial finding][initial-review]
was a P2 mismatch: both daily-workflow guides sent routine knowledge updates through

continuing that same read-only review was not a live-task retry.

Architecture, Work model, Workflow, CLI and Writing quality already own the delivered
facts. The [delegation/closeout Decision Record][decision] owns their rationale.
No new Spec or Decision Record is needed; the guide correction aligned existing
summaries. The whole Change records the delivered outcome and evidence boundary.

Document, named Change and diff checks passed. [Current readiness][ready] records
completionGate pass, archiveReady yes, Required 6/6, no incomplete Tasks/Verify,
active blockers or warnings. The CLI's semantic needs-review signal is separate
from actual independent review; deterministic readiness is not semantic approval.

CLI archived the Change and cleared Focus. Post-archive status has no open work.

## Limits

Acceptance is limited to the eleven selected standalone CLI scenarios. Matched
baseline/candidate comparisons, repeated reliability observations, broader judge
calibration and live natural-request/remaining-budget controls remain deferred.
Existing deterministic and historical evidence retains its own scope. These results
do not establish desktop-host equivalence, efficiency gains, general reliability,
publication permission or human acceptance.
