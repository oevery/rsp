### Protocol preservation: test-retention judgment remains disputed

Direct comparison confirms that the mixed-protocol controller and its two retained tests were unchanged. Native actions ran those tests and an additional temporary probe that included rejecting an unknown ID after valid IDs without sending. The assessment truthfully distinguished permanent tests from disposable verification. Product assertions, contract/scope and evidence/restraint all passed.

Only regression-value failed. The judge identified a concrete possible regression: silently dropping unknown IDs and sending to valid ones would escape both retained tests. That is a legitimate candidate for a small permanent test; it is not an error-text or source-style preference. However, the task asks for justified correction, preservation of correct behavior and proportionate additional checks without explicitly settling when an executed probe must become permanent. The absence of that permanent test does not establish a current product defect.

Keep the native 0.5 and treat the permanence obligation as a task/rubric alignment question requiring human assessment. Do not call the judge wrong merely because it requests coverage, or call the implementation wrong merely because a boundary lacks a permanent test.
