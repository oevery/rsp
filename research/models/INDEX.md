Cross-source models are added only when two or more completed source distillations support a useful synthesis. They are maintainer research, not RSP product truth. Record inputs as `<source>@<revision-or-snapshot-id> -> <report-path>`.

## Reuse current contracts, retain historical evidence

Research models preserve their dated mechanisms, dispositions and verdicts. Even an `accepted` model is not a current operating baseline. Before reuse, reconcile its recommendation with [Distribution](../../.rsp/specs/distribution.md), [Skill ownership](../../.rsp/specs/skill.md), and the current internal `author-rsp-skills`, `distill-upstream` and `release-acceptance` guidance. Current verification has two lanes; comparisons and holdouts are risk-selected research designs, and candidate handoff uses the smallest sufficient contract. Historical fixed behavior counts, mandatory holdouts and retired infrastructure do not regain authority through reuse.

- For managed Git inputs, read the exact cited report and revision evidence; use local upstream `status` to inspect the registered source's accepted/candidate state. Status does not establish model validity, authorize sync/accept or reconcile its recommendations with current contracts.
- For web snapshots, check the report's date, URLs, retained bytes, hashes, retrieval gaps and reuse limits. A matching hash proves retained identity, not current web freshness. Missing bytes leave retention unverified. New retrieval requires authorization; managed status cannot validate an unregistered snapshot.
- For either source type, check that cited evidence supports the selected inference and remains applicable to the current task. Neither a model's `complete` status nor intact source identity establishes freshness or present acceptance.
